Rice Leaf Diseases

Learn about the three main rice diseases our AI can detect

Rice Blast

Severe

One of the most destructive rice diseases worldwide, causing significant crop losses.

Symptoms: Brown or gray lesions with concentric rings and a whitish center on leaves and stems
Conditions: Spreads rapidly in cool, wet weather with high humidity
Control: Use disease-resistant varieties, proper field sanitation, and fungicide treatments

Bacterial Blight

Severe

A serious bacterial disease that can cause severe damage to rice crops in tropical regions.

Symptoms: Water-soaked yellow and white streaked lesions on leaves, often translucent
Conditions: High humidity and warm temperatures favor bacterial multiplication
Control: Plant resistant varieties and avoid overhead irrigation to reduce humidity

Rice Tungro

Moderate

A viral disease transmitted by insects that reduces grain yield and quality.

Symptoms: Yellowing and orange discoloration of leaves, plant stunting, and early ripening
Conditions: Transmitted by leafhoppers in warm tropical environments
Control: Use resistant varieties and control leafhopper vectors through insecticide application
